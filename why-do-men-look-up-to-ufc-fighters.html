Why do men look up to UFC fighters?

By The Falconer · 29 October 2026

What is it about observing a man knock someone out, just as he predicted, that lights a flame within us?

Could it be that glory, fame, and domination justify the pride we have in ourselves—that which is already present, waiting to be displayed for the world to see? An escape route for the inner workings of our ego, released through the adrenaline of a knockout. All of us must desire this release; few of us ever get the chance.

Victory seems to justify pride. When God denies it, is it really a defeat, or merely a lesson?
