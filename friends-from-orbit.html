Friends from Orbit?

By The Falconer · 29 October 2026

Why do women have the natural ability to spot the group leader? That single man just seems to pull all other men within his radius towards him. The sun: a blazing inferno, though also the guiding light the galaxy cannot survive without. Larger friends, whether in personality, physical size, or knowledge, seem to pull those close to them into their orbit.

In the same way, a weaker country often becomes the vassal of a larger neighbouring state. Over time, it slowly loses its own unique customs, traditions, and culture, eventually becoming a memory of the past. Men, like countries, appear to rise and fall. When in decline, they tend to orbit around those still burning with the passion of naïve youth and eventually implode.
