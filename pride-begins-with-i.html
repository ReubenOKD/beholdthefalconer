Pride Begins With I

By The Falconer · 1 November 2026

At times it can feel as if my God has left me; at others I am aware of the reality that I left Him.

Pride begins with "I", not with God.

You boasted of your works; you only riled up the enemy, not yourself.

How much time did you spend boasting? How much time did you spend thanking Him who created you?

You let anger stir you up, you allow hatred to steer you against the winds - have you forgotten who commands the winds? He Himself, the logos.

Even if you built an empire, you yourself would still be a sick man.
